# Launch as a free quota-limited beta

The first public release will have no billing: public evidence and generic precomputed Decision Checkpoints remain unlimited, while contextual AI generation starts with a configurable Generation Quota of five requests per User Account per day and fifty per month. User Account and Installation Token limits remain separately enforceable, Owners may adjust them, and exhausted or unavailable AI capacity falls back to deterministic checkpoints rather than blocking research.
