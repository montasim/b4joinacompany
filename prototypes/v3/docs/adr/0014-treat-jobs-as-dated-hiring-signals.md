# Treat jobs as dated Hiring Signals

The product will not claim to show live jobs: Hiring Signals expose their source and last-observed date, are recent when confirmed within 72 hours, become stale after seven days without confirmation, and become closed when removed at the source. High-interest and saved Company Identities refresh daily, the full dataset receives a weekly sweep, stale items leave the default view after 30 days but remain historical, and Salary Evidence retains separate role, location, source, and observation metadata.
