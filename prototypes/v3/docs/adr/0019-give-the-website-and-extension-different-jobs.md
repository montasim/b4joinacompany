# Give the website and extension different jobs

The Website and Extension share the Research API, Company Identities, Published Snapshots, evidence provenance, authentication, and applicable Workspace Data without sharing one presentation model. The Website is a deliberate decision workspace centered on Decision Checkpoints, comparisons, answers, and history. The Extension remains a fast, in-context Deshi Mula research overlay centered on company metrics, Stories, Hiring Signals, Salary Evidence, and cited questions; it must not become a miniature Website.
