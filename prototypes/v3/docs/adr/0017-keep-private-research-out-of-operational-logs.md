# Keep private research out of operational logs

Evidence Questions, Generated Answers, Evidence Bundles, notes, and answers remain Workspace Data and are never copied into ordinary operational logs. Operational Records retain only request and account identifiers, provider, model, timing, token usage, outcome, and citation counts for up to 30 days; provider errors are redacted, and private content reaches support only when a user explicitly attaches it to a case governed by audited Support Access.
