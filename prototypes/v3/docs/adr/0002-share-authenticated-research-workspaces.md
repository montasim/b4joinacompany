# Share authenticated Research Workspaces across clients

The BeforeJoin Website and Deshi Mula Extended Extension will authenticate the same User Account with Better Auth and synchronize one private Research Workspace through the shared Research API. Public registration will support Google sign-in and verified email/password with password recovery. MongoDB will hold authentication records and user-owned research state alongside—but logically separated from—the immutable published evidence; Installation Tokens remain only for pre-authentication quotas and abuse controls.
